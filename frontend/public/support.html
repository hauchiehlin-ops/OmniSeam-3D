Which formats work offline?

STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY, OFF, GLB/glTF and faceted STEP are processed on your device. Parametric STEP/IGES and native CAD/BIM formats (SolidWorks, Inventor, Rhino, IFC, DWG) need a cloud node.

The cloud node says it is starting

Free Hugging Face hardware sleeps after a period of inactivity and needs 1–3 minutes to wake up. Wait a moment and try again.

How do I create my own free node?

  1. Sign up for a free Hugging Face account.
  2. In the app open Engine Node → Step 1: Duplicate the node on Hugging Face, keep CPU Basic · Free hardware.
  3. When the Space shows Running, copy its direct URL (https://<user>-<space>.hf.space), paste it in the app, tap Test connection and then Save and use this node.

If the app says your node is outdated, open your Space settings on Hugging Face and choose Factory rebuild.

How do I save a converted file on iPhone or iPad?

Tap Download; the Share Sheet opens. Choose Save to Files, AirDrop, or a slicer app.

哪些格式可以離線處理？

STL、OBJ、3MF、PLY、OFF、GLB/glTF 與 Faceted STEP 會在裝置上處理。參數化 STEP/IGES 與原生 CAD/BIM 格式（SolidWorks、Inventor、Rhino、IFC、DWG）需要雲端節點。

雲端節點顯示「啟動中」

免費的 Hugging Face 硬體閒置一段時間後會休眠，喚醒需要 1–3 分鐘，請稍候再試。

如何建立自己的免費節點？

  1. 註冊免費的 Hugging Face 帳號。
  2. 在 App 中開啟「處理節點」→「步驟 1：在 Hugging Face 複製節點」，硬體保持 CPU Basic · Free。
  3. Space 顯示 Running 後，複製直接網址（https://<帳號>-<space>.hf.space）貼回 App，按「測試連線」再按「儲存並使用此節點」。

若 App 提示節點版本過舊，請到 Hugging Face 的 Space 設定中選擇「Factory rebuild」。

在 iPhone／iPad 上如何儲存轉換後的檔案？

按「下載」後會開啟分享面板，選擇「儲存到檔案」、AirDrop 或切片軟體即可。
