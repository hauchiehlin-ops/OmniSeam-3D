OmniSeam 3D Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Applies to the OmniSeam 3D iOS app

Summary

  • By default, your 3D files are processed on your device and never leave it.
  • Cloud processing is optional. Before the first upload to a cloud node, the app asks for your consent.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Files uploaded to the OmniSeam public demo node are deleted automatically within 1 hour.

1. Local mode (default)

Standard formats such as STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY, OFF, GLB/glTF and faceted STEP are opened, inspected, repaired and converted inside the app on your device. In this mode no file content is sent over the network.

2. Cloud mode (optional)

Some formats (for example parametric STEP/IGES, SolidWorks, Inventor, Rhino, IFC, DWG) require a processing node. You can choose:

2.1 Public demo node

Operated by the OmniSeam 3D developer on Hugging Face Spaces (Hugging Face, Inc. is the hosting provider). When you choose it and confirm the upload prompt, the selected file is sent over HTTPS. The node stores the uploaded file and the converted result temporarily so you can download it. A cleanup process deletes uploads and results within 1 hour. We do not use the files for any other purpose and do not share them with third parties other than the hosting provider. The hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) under its own policy.

2.2 Your own node

You can create your own node in your Hugging Face account. Files are then sent only to that node. The OmniSeam 3D developer has no access to it; data handling there is governed by you and by Hugging Face's terms of service and privacy policy.

3. Data we collect

DataWhenPurposeLinked to you / tracking
3D model files you choose to uploadOnly in cloud mode, after your consentApp functionality (conversion and repair)Not linked to your identity · not used for tracking

The app stores your settings (language, selected node URL, consent choice) locally on your device only.

3.1 App configuration and time check

When it starts or returns to the foreground, the app downloads a small public settings file from this website (public node address and announcements) and reads the server's current time from the same request, to check the 7-day Pro trial. No file content, identifier or personal data is sent.

3.2 In-App Purchases

The Pro purchase and the free trial are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. The app reads the purchase status from Apple on your device to unlock Pro features.

4. Permissions

The iOS app opens files only when you pick them, or when you share them to the app from Files or another app. It saves results only through the system Share Sheet. AR preview uses Apple's AR Quick Look on your device.

5. Children

The app is a general-purpose engineering tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

  • Use local mode to keep all files on your device.
  • Decline the upload prompt at any time; nothing is uploaded.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored settings. One value is kept in the device Keychain so that reinstalling does not restart the free trial: the latest date the app has observed. It contains no personal data.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date above. Questions: dr.cobra.lin@gmail.com · Support

OmniSeam 3D 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 14 日 · 適用於 OmniSeam 3D iOS App

摘要

  • 預設情況下，你的 3D 檔案在你的裝置上處理，不會離開裝置。
  • 雲端處理為可選功能。第一次上傳到雲端節點前，App 會先徵求你的同意。
  • 不需要註冊帳號，也不使用任何分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。
  • 上傳到 OmniSeam 公用示範節點的檔案會在 1 小時內自動刪除。

1. 本地模式（預設）

STL、OBJ、3MF、PLY、OFF、GLB/glTF 與 Faceted STEP 等標準格式，會在你的裝置上開啟、檢查、修復與轉換。此模式不會透過網路傳送任何檔案內容。

2. 雲端模式（可選）

部分格式（例如參數化 STEP/IGES、SolidWorks、Inventor、Rhino、IFC、DWG）需要處理節點。你可以選擇：

2.1 公用示範節點

由 OmniSeam 3D 開發者架設於 Hugging Face Spaces（主機服務商為 Hugging Face, Inc.）。當你選擇並同意上傳後，所選檔案會透過 HTTPS 傳送。節點會暫存上傳檔與轉換結果供你下載，清理程序會在 1 小時內刪除。我們不會將檔案用於其他用途，也不會提供給主機服務商以外的第三方。主機服務商可能依其政策保留一般技術紀錄（例如 IP 位址與請求時間）。

2.2 你自己的節點

你可以在自己的 Hugging Face 帳號中建立節點，之後檔案只會送到該節點。OmniSeam 3D 開發者無法存取該節點；其資料處理由你與 Hugging Face 的服務條款及隱私權政策規範。

3. 我們收集的資料

資料時機用途是否連結身分／追蹤
你選擇上傳的 3D 模型檔僅在雲端模式且經你同意後App 功能（轉換與修復）不連結身分 · 不用於追蹤

App 只在你的裝置上儲存設定（語言、選擇的節點網址、同意選項）。

3.1 App 設定與時間確認

App 啟動或回到前景時，會從本網站下載一個小型公開設定檔（公用節點網址與公告），並從同一個請求讀取伺服器目前的時間，用來確認專業版 7 天試用期。不會傳送任何檔案內容、識別碼或個人資料。

3.2 App 內購買

專業版購買與免費試用完全由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理，我們不會收到你的付款資料或 Apple ID。App 會在你的裝置上向 Apple 讀取購買狀態，以開啟專業版功能。

4. 權限

iOS App 只會開啟你選擇的檔案，或你從「檔案」及其他 App 分享進來的檔案；轉換結果只透過系統分享面板儲存。AR 預覽使用裝置上的 Apple AR Quick Look。

5. 兒童

本 App 為一般用途的工程工具，並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

6. 你的選擇

  • 使用本地模式，所有檔案都留在裝置上。
  • 隨時可以拒絕上傳提示，不會上傳任何檔案。
  • 刪除 App 即會移除所有本機設定。唯一例外是裝置鑰匙圈（Keychain）中的一個數值：App 觀察到的最晚日期，用來避免重裝後重新開始免費試用。這個數值不含任何個人資料。
